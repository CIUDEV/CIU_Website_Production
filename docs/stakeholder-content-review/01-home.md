## Who We Are

**Label:** WHO WE ARE  
**Heading:** Faith, Knowledge and Community

The Canadian Islamic Union is a registered charity organization dedicated to strengthening Muslim individuals and families through education, mentorship, spiritual development, charitable initiatives, and community service.

We aim to create a welcoming environment where people of all ages can deepen their understanding of Islam, build meaningful relationships, and contribute positively to Canadian society.

**Button:** Learn More About CIU

---

## Guided by Purpose

**Heading:** Guided by Purpose  
**Subheading:** Our work is centred around four areas that support individuals, families, and the wider community.

1. **Islamic Education** — Providing accessible and authentic Islamic learning for children, youth, adults, and families.
2. **Mentorship** — Supporting personal, spiritual, and leadership development through qualified teachers and mentors.
3. **Family and Community** — Creating welcoming spaces where families can connect, grow, and support one another.
4. **Service and Charity** — Organizing charitable initiatives, outreach projects, and volunteer opportunities that benefit the community.
