## Hands-On Service

**Label:** Hands-On Service  
**Heading:** Volunteer for Community Work

Volunteering with CIU is for people who want to contribute time and effort on-site — helping with programs, events, facility support, and service to families across the GTA.

This is separate from CIU membership, which is our email list for community updates. If you want to receive announcements only, join membership instead.

**Highlights:**
- Masjid and facility support at the centre
- Event coordination and community program setup
- Education, youth, and family initiative support
- High school volunteer hours and senior volunteer roles

## Volunteer Opportunities

**Heading:** Volunteer Opportunities  
**Subheading:** Explore current ways to serve — from general community support to senior roles and high school volunteer hours.

### Volunteers Needed
General volunteer roles at the centre — outreach, program setup, family events, facility care, and day-to-day community support.

### Senior Volunteers
Enthusiastic, social seniors who want to give back through community engagement, event support, and sharing wisdom with younger generations.

### High School Volunteer Hours
Students completing required volunteer hours through admin support, mentoring, event coordination, and program assistance. *(Poster mentions 40 hours with Azhar Canada and CIU)*

## Volunteer Registration Form

**Heading:** Volunteer Registration  
**Description:** Tell us how you would like to serve at the masjid and centre. Our team will follow up about current volunteer needs and scheduling.

**Form fields:**
- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Volunteer Category: High School Student · Adult · Senior
- How Would You Like to Help? (select all):
  - Event Coordination
  - Community Engagement
  - Education & Kids Programs
  - Masjid & Facility Support
  - Outreach & Family Support
  - Admin & Office Support
  - Student Mentoring
- Availability: Weekdays · Weekends · Evenings · Flexible
- Completing High School Volunteer Hours? Yes · No (for student volunteers)
- Experience & Notes (optional)
- Agreement: I understand this form is for hands-on volunteer service at CIU and I agree to be contacted about volunteer opportunities.

**Submit button:** Submit Volunteer Registration
